# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web (marketing site, `landing-page/`, Astro, EN and FR). The product itself is iOS and macOS.

## Users

French-speaking professionals who spend their day in conversations: freelancers, consultants, small companies. They want to win time and earn more. Some work alone, some in teams, some are hiring: "without hiring" is a bonus, never a condition. Secondary: people who already run their own Hermes agent, and teams that want a human to help them start.

## Product Purpose

FlowFlow turns what people say into notes they can search, share and question, on iPhone and Mac. Success on the site: a professional understands within seconds what it does in their day, downloads it, or books 30 minutes with Mirko.

## Positioning

One app that hears you, writes, sorts, answers with its sources, and links to the agent you host yourself. Free for now with your own keys, open source, transcription that can stay on the device, and sync with no server in the middle.

## Operating Context

Car parks after a client meeting. A meeting room with no laptop open. Two minutes before a call. An evening when the agent takes over. Phones in one hand, voice instead of typing.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Shipped in 2.1.2: dictation, long recordings, cloud or local Whisper, meetings with chapters and dated summaries, chat with cited sources, themes, reminders on iPhone, LAN sync, Hermes link with approvals and scheduled tasks. Spaces, sharing, connectors, agents and Hermes notifications need premium, granted by hand. No price, no checkout. All AI needs the user's keys or a ChatGPT subscription. Claude subscription login is debug only. Free is free for now, never promised forever.

## Brand Commitments

Name FlowFlow. Logo: glossy orange pretzel-gear with two googly eyes. Headline stays "À portée de voix" / "A word away" and "Parle, FlowFlow exécute" / "Speak, FlowFlow runs it". French copy uses "tu". Orange belongs to the brand. Typefaces and surface colours may be redefined.

## Evidence on Hand

Real captures and clips on demo data in `screenshots/2.1.1/` and `~/code/flowflow-videos/`, promo in 35 s EN and FR, 172 GitHub stars, EUPL licence, Awesome Dioxus listing. No customer testimonial, no App Store rating, no logo wall, no measured time saved: none may be fabricated. No Mac capture and no video of a meeting exist yet.

## Product Principles

- Show the app working before explaining it.
- Say what happens in the visitor's day, in their words.
- Promise only what the version on sale does.
- Say plainly what leaves the device and what it costs.
- Welcome solo workers and teams equally.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard focus, reduced-motion fallbacks, captions or text equivalents for video, usable at iPhone width.
